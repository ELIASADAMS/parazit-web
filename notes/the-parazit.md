The PARAZIT is an invader-project. The innate features of the PARAZIT art group are creative expansion and replication of gallery branches in the alien territories, with or without consent of the owners. Since its foundation the PARAZIT has no legal address, but uses the addresses of others. (From the PARAZIT group manifesto).
In a manner of speaking the PARAZIT creates naïve art but outside figurative art forms. The particular nature of the group artists reveals an articulated artistic statement and independence from the borders and formalities of any kind. It is unlikely anyone would dare to call the PARAZIT’s works typical for present art scene. And it’s wonderful. In times of universalized system where the topical nature of the concept takes on almost maniacal character and commercialization of art becomes its dominant characteristic works by the PARAZIT group artistic has its own self-irony. 
The PARAZIT group art practice cannot be brought to one single vector. It is discrete and avoids unification. If we can speak of any strategy the group has that would be a strategy to resist any definitions or generalizations. And it is a successful strategy. From that point of view the PARAZIT’s routine looks like a cloud technology — it is a variety of data spread along different locations and connections between this data that are constantly appearing and disappearing so the whole picture would change. But these constant changes – one after another and so on - are in the end the process and the result of their work. The PARAZIT based on a cloud principle where there are both permanent participants, temporary allies for one-two events, gallery spaces and not-gallery spaces taken by surprise by art that has appeared in them, conscious and unconscious audience.
In 2000 artists Vadim Flyagin and Vladimir Kozin founded a new cultural institution in Saint-Petersburg that was called the Parazit Gallery. The author of that name is Vadim Flyagin. After some successful exhibition in 2002 due to the departure of Vadim Flyagin to his motherland in Nizhny Novgorod the Parazit was closed. 
In 2004 by the will and mutual agreement and cooperation of Vladimir Kozin, Yury Nikiforov, Igor Mezheritzkiy (leaders of three groups - the “New Dumps” Comradeship, the “Poligon” and the “Tizholava Izkustva”) the gallery was reactivated.  Artists set quiet a demanding schedule for themselves changing exhibitions every two weeks that they has been keeping up until today. After joining of active and creative youth the Parazit becomes an informal creative community of artists where the difference in age between its members can be more than 40 years. For 10 years of the Parazit existence many well-known groups has appeared, taken a start and made a name for themselves like the Protez (prosthetic device), the Mylo (soap), the Mir (world and peace), REPA and the others. And the whole number of exhibitions and their participants is beyond all computation.
